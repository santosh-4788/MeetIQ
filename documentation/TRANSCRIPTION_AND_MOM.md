| Part | Status |
|---|---|

## Minutes of meeting without AI (built)

**Where:** a "Minutes of meeting" card on the meeting page, which opens `/meetings/:id/minutes`.

### Fields
- **From the meeting record (cannot be typed over):**
  - meeting ID and title; category;
  - date; scheduled and actual start and end; actual duration;
  - host and organiser;
  - participants, each marked "Attended" or "Did not join" (from `mtg.MeetingAttendance`);
  - customer meetings only: customer name and Prarambh Main ID.
- **Typed by people:**
  - discussion summary (up to 8000 characters);
  - customer requirements (offered for customer meetings);
  - decisions; pending activities; follow-up date.
- **Action items** (up to 50): description; assigned employee (active employees only); due date; priority Low/Medium/High; status Open/In progress/Done.
- Empty sections are shown and printed as **"Not specified"**. Nothing is filled in automatically beyond the meeting record.

### Who may do what

| Action | Who |
|---|---|
| Write and edit a draft | The host, the organiser, a co-host, or a user with `meetings.view.all` |
| Read a draft and its history | The people above, plus approvers |
| Approve | Permission `minutes.approve` (Super Admin, Admin, Department Head, Team Leader; changeable in Roles & permissions) |
| Read approved minutes | Everyone who can see the meeting |
| Reopen approved minutes | Writers or approvers, with a reason |

**Customer meetings** also need `customer.meetings.view` for any of the above.

### Workflow
1. **Draft:** possible once the meeting has started (`LIVE`, `COMPLETED` or `INCOMPLETE`).
2. **Save:** every save creates a new version.
   - A save based on an older version is refused (409), so two people cannot overwrite each other.
3. **Approve:**
   - the minutes become read-only and visible to every participant;
   - empty minutes cannot be approved.
4. **Reopen:** needs a reason. The minutes go back to draft and need approval again. The approved version stays in the history.

### Records and output
- **History:** `mtg.MeetingMinutesVersions` keeps a full snapshot of every version (created, saved, approved, reopened), with who and when.
- **Audit:** `MINUTES_CREATED`, `MINUTES_SAVED`, `MINUTES_APPROVED` and `MINUTES_REOPENED`. They record the meeting ID and version, never the text.
- **Print / Save as PDF:** the browser's print dialog, with a clean A4 layout.
  - All languages print correctly, Hindi included.
  - No PDF library or font file is needed, and nothing is uploaded.
  - The footer states that the minutes were written by people and not generated by AI.
- **Sharing:** nothing is sent to anyone automatically. People share the PDF themselves.
  - Sending approved minutes by e-mail from the app, and DOCX download, were not built. Each can be added as a further option.

## Transcription (proposed, not built)
- **Engine:** whisper.cpp (MIT), local and CPU-only.
  - Model size (`base` / `small` / `medium`) to be chosen from **measured** speed and accuracy on this server.
  - English and Hindi; Hindi-English mixed speech evaluated on real recordings.
- **Input:** the meeting's verified recording. Audio is extracted with the existing FFmpeg.
  - Unrecorded meetings have no source audio, so post-meeting transcription is not possible for them.
  - Live captions would need a separate, heavier design.
- **Output:**
  - timestamped segments; `[unclear]` marks;
  - neutral speaker labels;
  - corrections by the host are saved as a new version.

## AI-drafted minutes (proposed, not built)
- **Where:** the AI would only fill a **draft** in the same minutes editor described above, marked "AI Generated – Pending Human Review". The same approval workflow applies.
- **Content:** anything not said in the meeting is written as **"Not specified – Please review."** Employees, dates and commitments are never invented.
